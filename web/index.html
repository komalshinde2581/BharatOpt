BharatOpt-X | Sovereign Optimization Architecture
🇮🇳 Sovereign Optimization Core Architecture

BharatOpt-X is built as a sovereign alternative to commercial solvers for Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Limited (MRPL) and the Smart India Hackathon (SIH), avoiding dependencies on third-party optimization engines.

Key Subsystem Engines:

  • Two-Phase Revised Simplex: Native sparse matrix representation (CSR/CSC) with LU-factorization.
  • Branch & Cut (MILP): Strong branching heuristic coupled with a Graph Neural Network (GNN) variable selector.
  • Primal-Dual Interior Point (IPM): Cholesky factorization for convex Quadratic Programming (QP).
  • GPU ADMM & CUDA: Deeply integrated PyTorch data-routing pipeline for heavy industrial LPs.
